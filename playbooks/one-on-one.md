# One-on-One Meeting Playbook

## 🎯 Purpose
One-on-one meetings are a critical tool for building trust, coaching engineers, and surfacing issues early.  
They are not status updates—they are about **people, growth, and alignment**.

---

## 🗓️ Logistics & Preparation
- Cadence: Weekly (20-30 mins) or Bi-weekly (45–60 mins) depending on the engineer's seniority and current project pressure.
- Each report has a private, shared document. Both of us add agenda items throughout the week to avoid "surprises" and allow for reflection.
- I  spend 10 minutes before each session reviewing the report's recent PRs, architectural contributions (ADRs), and previous action items.

---

## 📑 Agenda Template
1. **Check-in**
   - How are you feeling this week?
   - Any wins or challenges you’d like to share?

2. **Growth & Development**
   - Skills you want to build
   - Feedback on recent work
   - Career goals and progress

3. **Team & Project Health**
   - Collaboration challenges
   - Dependencies or blockers
   - Suggestions for improving team processes

4. **Manager Feedback**
   - What can I do differently to support you?
   - Any feedback on leadership or communication?

---

## ✅ Best Practices
- **Listen more than you talk**: Aim for 70% employee, 30% manager speaking time.
- **Document outcomes**: Keep lightweight notes for continuity.
- **Follow up**: Action items should be tracked and revisited.
- **Create safety**: Encourage candor by being open and non-defensive.

---

## 🚀 Example Questions
- What’s one thing that energized you this week?
- What’s blocking you from doing your best work?
- How do you feel about the team’s current direction?
- What skills do you want to develop in the next 6 months?
