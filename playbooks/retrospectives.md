# Retrospectives Playbook

## 🎯 Purpose
Retrospectives help teams reflect on their work, celebrate wins, and identify improvements.  
They are a cornerstone of continuous improvement and psychological safety.

---

## 🗓️ Cadence
- At the end of each sprint or major milestone
- 60–90 minutes depending on team size
- Facilitated by EM, TPM, or Scrum Master

---

## 📑 Agenda Template
1. **Set the Stage**
   - Remind the team: this is a safe space, not a blame session.
   - Share the goal: learn and improve together.

2. **Gather Data**
   - What went well?
   - What didn’t go well?
   - Any surprises or risks?

3. **Generate Insights**
   - Look for patterns in feedback.
   - Discuss root causes, not just symptoms.

4. **Decide Actions**
   - Pick 2–3 concrete improvements for the next cycle.
   - Assign owners and timelines.

5. **Close**
   - Appreciate contributions.
   - End on a positive note.

---

## ✅ Best Practices
- **Rotate facilitators**: Builds shared ownership.
- **Visualize feedback**: Use sticky notes, Miro boards, or whiteboards.
- **Limit action items**: Focus on a few impactful changes.
- **Follow through**: Review previous retro actions at the start of the next one.

---

## 🚀 Example Techniques
- **Start/Stop/Continue**: Simple framework for feedback.
- **Mad/Sad/Glad**: Emotional check-in to surface hidden concerns.
- **4Ls (Liked, Learned, Lacked, Longed for)**: Encourages deeper reflection.
