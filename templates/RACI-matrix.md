# RACI Matrix Template

## 🎯 Purpose
A RACI matrix clarifies **roles and responsibilities** across stakeholders for each deliverable or activity.  
It ensures accountability, avoids confusion, and improves cross‑team collaboration.

---

## 📑 Structure

| Task / Deliverable       | Responsible (R)       | Accountable (A) | Consulted (C)                  | Informed (I)          |
|--------------------------|-----------------------|-----------------|--------------------------------|-----------------------|
| Requirements Gathering   | TPM                   | Product Manager | UX Lead, Engineering Lead      | Engineering Team      |
| Architecture Design      | Engineering Lead      | EM              | TPM, Security Team             | Product Manager       |
| Development              | Engineers             | EM              | TPM, QA Lead                   | Stakeholders          |
| Testing & QA             | QA Lead               | EM              | Engineers                      | TPM, Product Manager  |
| Deployment               | DevOps Engineer       | EM              | TPM, Security Team             | All Stakeholders      |
| Post‑Launch Monitoring   | TPM                   | EM              | Engineers, QA Lead             | Product Manager       |

---

## ✅ Best Practices
- **One “A” per task**: Only one person should be accountable to avoid ambiguity.  
- **Balance workload**: Ensure responsibilities are distributed fairly.  
- **Update regularly**: Revise the matrix as scope or team composition changes.  
- **Communicate clearly**: Share the matrix with all stakeholders at project kickoff.  
- **Use for alignment**: Review in program syncs to resolve ownership disputes early.  

---

## 🚀 Usage
- Create a new RACI matrix for each major project or initiative.  
- Review during project planning and update at key milestones.  
- Use it alongside the **Project Plan** and **Risk Register** for complete alignment.
