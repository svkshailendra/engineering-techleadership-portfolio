## 🧭 Leadership in Practice

As a Technology Lead, I have operated beyond pure technical execution, taking ownership of team outcomes, delivery reliability, and cross-functional alignment.

My responsibilities have included:

- Leading teams of 8–12 engineers across development and production support, ensuring consistent delivery and system stability.  
- Driving key technical and process decisions, particularly around reliability, monitoring, and incident management.  
- Working closely with architects, product managers, and business stakeholders to translate requirements into scalable solutions. 
- Improving delivery predictability through structured sprint coordination and zero-downtime release management, while driving proactive monitoring initiatives that reduced ticket volume and improved incident response times.

This repository reflects the frameworks, decisions, and practices I’ve used in these areas.
